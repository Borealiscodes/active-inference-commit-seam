## 2. Anatomy of LLM Misattribution: Tool Conflation & Phantom Provenance

When LLMs assist in synthesizing interdisciplinary documents—spanning legal statutes, differential geometry, and software documentation—they exhibit three predictable failure modes:

* **Citation & Entity Blending:** Because transformer attention mechanisms optimize for semantic coherence across co-occurring context windows, a library cited for *vector math* (`CAUSA`) can be smoothly woven into prose discussing *legal causation*, creating the false impression that the software or its author validates the legal thesis.
* **Synthetic Annotation Layering:** LLMs frequently invent connective tissue ("annotation layers" or unified pipeline narratives) that links independent repositories into a single continuous mechanism, erasing licensing boundaries and distinct design scopes.
* **Epistemic Promotion (`DERIVED` $\to$ `OBSERVED`):** Without hard type enforcement, a model's interpretative summary of a source document is treated as equivalent to a verbatim primary record.

As Stell (2026) explicitly warns in the `CAUSA` documentation:
> *"A high score is not truthfulness. Congruence between expression and state means the two moved together. An agent can be congruently wrong."*

Treating an internal geometric or causal metric as proof of external legal truth is a category error—one that deterministic gates are specifically engineered to block.

## 3. Strengthening RFC-006 with Clean-Room Deterministic Gates (`deterministic-ai-gate`)

To make legal and human-rights documentation pipelines forensically defensible, external evidentiary records must pass through a zero-dependency deterministic gate (`deterministic-ai-gate`) *before* any geometric or causal modeling occurs:

1. **Stage 1 — Pre-LLM Cryptographic Anchoring (`SHA-256`):** Primary legal filings, administrative notices, and treaty texts are split into discrete chunks $\{c_0, \dots, c_{n-1}\}$ and hashed prior to model exposure:
   $$h_i = \text{SHA-256}(c_i)$$
2. **Stage 2 — Bounded Parser Extraction:** A language model acts strictly as a schema-constrained parser, prohibited from inferring missing dates, computing totals, or paraphrasing direct quotes.
3. **Stage 3 — Fail-Closed CPU Verification:**
   * **Verbatim Provenance Check:** Every extracted quote $q$ must match a pre-committed chunk hash ($h_i$) or verified substring ($q \subseteq c_i$ with $\text{SHA-256}(c_i) = h_i$). Any AI-altered paraphrase or blended citation triggers immediate rejection (`ProvenanceError`).
   * **Exact Rational Arithmetic ($\mathbb{Q}_B$):** Statutory deadlines, day counts, and fee ratios are evaluated as irreducible fractions $p/q$ bounded by a 2048-bit precision ceiling, eliminating IEEE-754 rounding drift.
   * **Chronological DAG Acyclicity:** Procedural timelines (e.g., filing $\to$ notice $\to$ appeal $\to$ dismissal) are verified via Kahn's topological sort (Kahn, 1962), rejecting circular or retroactive date contradictions.
